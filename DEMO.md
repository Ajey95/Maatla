# Maatla demo sequence

1. Open the seller workspace and select the synthetic buyer. Point to the dated prior-call concern and budget. Say these are stored examples, not field research.
2. Create a room and open the buyer link in a second browser. The buyer sees a consent checkbox and a plain call screen; the seller sees the private coaching workspace.
3. With two microphones/headsets, have the Hinglish buyer speak naturally about budget, plot size, road access, registration papers, and a family site visit. The transcript should identify the buyer. The seller immediately sees a provisional Telugu gist for each speech segment; adjacent segments combine into a final turn cue after a pause.
4. Read the private Telugu text cue. Its audio is optional. The seller's outgoing microphone starts muted. Edit the Hinglish draft, check its updated Telugu meaning, and approve the Hinglish Timbre voice for the buyer. Leave a short gap before the next buyer turn.
5. Search an approved source or fetch an allowlisted official page. Show its title, URL, and fetch time. Explain that it does not verify any particular plot's title.
6. Review a proposed client fact, approve it, and add a dated commitment. Edit the suggested Hinglish reply, wait for the Telugu meaning beneath it to refresh, and approve both versions; Gnani Timbre plays the Hinglish text to the buyer.
7. End the call and show the extractive summary. Create a new room for the same buyer to demonstrate continuity and the prior-call context.

The **Run sample** control is a backup walkthrough when microphones or connectivity are unavailable. It creates clearly labelled synthetic text turns. Do not claim that control demonstrates live STT or audio latency.

## What to measure in a real two-device test

- Time from buyer speech end to Gnani transcript.
- Time from transcript to immediate cue.
- Time from transcript to translated meaning and refined suggestion.
- Time from seller approval to buyer TTS playback.
- WebRTC audio connectivity with and without configured TURN on different networks.
- Accuracy with Telugu-English and Hindi-English code-mixed speakers, names, plot units, and property terms.

Record the measured values and the test audio conditions before making performance claims.
